# Combat Feel — Blind Judging Criteria

## Judging Question

Bursts through a full combo landing on a target. Which hits feel heavier and more satisfying?

## Pair Descriptions

**Pair 01:** A full-combo motion sequence showing three consecutive punches/hits landing on a single target, with visible feedback progression from first to final hit.

**Pair 02:** A single impact moment where a punch connects with an enemy, showing hitstop flash, damage feedback, and initial knockback.

**Pair 03:** A third-hit impact moment showing the finisher blow connecting and its visual feedback (flash, knockback distance, particle effects).
