# Judging question

Frame strips of the player character idling, running, dashing and doing its attack combo, plus one still of it in its world. Which character feels more alive and appealing?

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie` (a tie only if genuinely indistinguishable in quality).

Each strip is 18 frames at 10 fps, read left to right, top to bottom, each frame a 320x240 crop that follows the character at its normal in-game scale.

# Pairs

1. Both panels: the character standing idle, 18 frames at 10 fps.
2. Both panels: the character running and changing direction, 18 frames at 10 fps.
3. Both panels: the character dashing sideways, before, during and after the dash, 18 frames at 10 fps.
4. Both panels: the character performing its three-hit melee attack combo, 18 frames at 10 fps.
5. Both panels: a 640x360 still of the character standing in a level at in-game scale.
