# HUD and in-game UI Blind Comparison Criteria

## Judging Question

Mid-fight stills showing idle state and active combat. Which HUD looks more polished and readable?

## Pair Descriptions

**Pair 01:** Idle HUD state during early gameplay—player not under immediate threat, full health, no active effects. Evaluate visual clarity, element positioning, and how well the interface reads at a glance without causing eye fatigue.

**Pair 02:** Mid-combat HUD during engagement with enemies. Evaluate responsiveness of HUD updates, readability of health/resource states during action, information hierarchy, and whether the UI supports or distracts from moment-to-moment gameplay.
