# Blind Judging Criteria: Combat Feel

## Judging Question
**Bursts through a full combo landing on a target. Which hits feel heavier and more satisfying?**

## Pair Descriptions

**Pair 01 (Motion Strip):**
A contact sheet showing a full sequence of attacks landing on enemy targets, capturing the progression of the combo and how targets respond to being hit repeatedly.

**Pair 02 (Impact Moment):**
The critical frame where a hit connects with a target—the moment of impact showing the flash, sparkle effects, and initial target response.

**Pair 03 (Aftermath):**
The frames immediately following impact, showing how the target staggers, recoils, or moves in response to the hit and how quickly it recovers.
