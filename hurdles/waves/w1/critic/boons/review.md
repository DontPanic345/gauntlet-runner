PIN: https://poncle.itch.io/vampire-survivors | 4.8 stars, 1652 ratings | The strongest fitting HTML5, browser-playable, top-rated (itch.io top-rated pool) roguelite whose core loop is exactly this piece's job: kill things, hit a pick-1-of-N upgrade screen, watch the upgrades visibly change how you fight. It originated as a free itch.io HTML5 game (still playable there), so it can be captured live rather than from a trailer.

## Verdict (not blind)

Ours loses: the upgrade choices themselves read fine once settled, but the shrine takes far too long to become legible and the reference's swarm-driven combat reads as more exciting at a glance.

## Biggest gap

The shrine's card reveal animation is broken/way too slow: for 3-8 seconds after `?showcase=boons` loads (confirmed across many repeated runs at 500ms through 5000ms wait times), the three cards sit in an ugly dithered black/white checkerboard noise state, and sometimes render as flat blank-white rectangles, before finally resolving into the actual card art around the 6-8 second mark. A player or judge who screenshots "the moment" during this long window — which is most of it — sees what looks like a texture-load glitch, not "beautiful cards." Fix the flip-in to be fast (under 500ms) and to interpolate through something intentional (a card-back-to-face flip, a wipe, a fade) instead of raw noise dithering.

## Problems

1. **Card reveal is slow and glitchy** (see Biggest gap). Evidence: `/tmp` captures at wait=500/1000/1500/2000/2500/3000/4000/4200/4500/5000ms via `node tools/capture.cjs --url "/?showcase=boons"` all show either growing card-back rectangles, a black/white checkerboard dissolve, or (once, at 3200ms wait in a second run) solid blank-white cards — never the finished card face before ~6s. Captured in `ours/01-choice-screen.png` only because I deliberately waited 8s; the packet does not show the actual first impression a player gets. This single issue undercuts must_have #2 ("the choice screen is a moment ... cards animate in") the most: right now the moment is a long stall through visual noise, not a reveal.
2. **Hover/focus feedback is nearly invisible.** Pressing A/D to browse cards (the documented control, per the on-screen "A D BROWSE" hint) produces no visible highlight, scale, glow, or lift on the focused card — I compared three consecutive frames at the settled state (`/tmp/boons_shots/hovertest_strip.png`) and could not find any pixel difference tied to the browse key. The only hover cue I found at all was a faint dotted ellipse under a card, and it only appeared during the self-playing demo bot's own auto-selection, or immediately on a raw mouse click that instantly took the card (no separate hover state observed). Compare to the reference, where the hovered/selected weapon row is unambiguous. must_have #2 says hover must "feel tactile"; right now it barely registers as a state at all when driven by keyboard, which is how critics and many players will actually browse.
3. **The reference's combat reads as far more exciting at equal zoom.** `hurdles/waves/w1/critic/boons/ref/04-dramatic-effect.png` (a real screenshot from the game's own itch.io page) shows hundreds of enemies, a weapon effect sweeping the whole screen, and dozens of stacked damage numbers. Our best equivalent (`ours/04-dramatic-effect.png`, Storm Call at level 1 in the dummy room) has one hero, five enemies, and a single lightning burst. Some of this gap is genre (Vampire Survivors is a bullet-heaven built around enemy count scaling), but our dummy-room showcase caps enemy count low even for a "boons in action" demo — the showcase should spawn a denser wave so a boon's AoE and chain effects have more to visibly chew through.
4. **Pickup vacuum works but is easy to miss.** `strip_lodestone.png` (not in the blind packet, kept as supporting evidence) shows shards streaking toward the hero with a cyan trail — this is real and satisfies must_have #3 for the *visual* half. I could not evaluate the "sound ladder" half at all; the critic has no audio capture step in this workflow and the game contract doesn't expose one for the boons piece. Flag this as unverified, not failing.
5. **Card back-face art is generic.** Even after the reveal finishes, the card frame, glow, and back pattern are the same for all three cards regardless of rarity until the flip completes — only then do the border colors (frost/cyan/gold per `RARITY`) show up. Because the reveal is slow (problem 1), for most of the shrine's visible lifetime all three cards look identical and rarity is not conveyed. Once revealed, the rarity border color scheme is fine and reasonably distinct.

## must_have checklist

1. **"every boon changes something you can SEE in combat, not just a number"** — PASS. Verified live via `?showcase=boons&give=<id>` for chain-spark, ember-trail, orbit-blades, storm-call, phoenix-spark, volatile-soul, and lodestone: each produced a distinct, readable visual (lightning arcs, ground fire trail, orbiting blades, sky-strike burst, red-flash revive, explosion rings, magnetized shard trails respectively). See `/tmp` strips referenced above and `ours/03-boons-in-action-strip.png` / `ours/04-dramatic-effect.png`.
2. **"the choice screen is a moment: cards animate in, hover and select feel tactile"** — FAIL. Cards do animate in, but the animation is slow (3-8s) and passes through a broken-looking checkerboard/blank-white state (problem 1); hover feedback is effectively absent under keyboard control (problem 2). Select (press Enter/J to take a card) does transition cleanly into gameplay with a readable banner, so the "select" half is closer to fine than "hover."
3. **"pickups vacuum toward the player with a sound ladder"** — PARTIAL. Vacuum motion verified visually (lodestone boon, and the base pickup radius in the dummy room). Sound ladder unverifiable by this critic (no audio capture available in this workflow); do not treat as failing, but it is unconfirmed.
4. **"synergies exist between at least 4 pairs of boons"** — NOT DIRECTLY VERIFIED. `boons.js` defines a `synergiesFor` function and the offer-weighting formula gives roughly 2.2x weight to boons that are in an active synergy, which implies synergy pairs exist in data, but I did not visually confirm 4 distinct synergy pairs producing a combined effect on screen (this would need targeted `&give=id1,id2` captures per pair and was out of time budget this wave). Flag for the next critique pass: capture `?showcase=boons&give=id1,id2` for each declared synergy pair and confirm a visible combined effect or at least a UI synergy callout.
