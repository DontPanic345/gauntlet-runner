# Judging criteria — hero

Question: Frame strips of idle, run, dash, attack combo, hurt, death. Which character
feels more alive and appealing?

Each pair shows the same kind of moment for both sides, in this order:

1. **Idle** — the player character standing alone, at rest, weapon in hand.
2. **Run** — a 4-frame motion strip sampled from a burst of the character moving
   sideways, evenly spaced in time.
3. **Attack** — the character mid-attack, weapon extended toward a target, in the
   anticipation/strike part of the swing.
4. **Hurt** — the character at the moment of taking damage, amid a hit reaction.
5. **Death** — the character's death or defeat beat, as the game itself presents it.
