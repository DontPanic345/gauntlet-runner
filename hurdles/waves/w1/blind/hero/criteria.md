# Blind comparison: hero model and animation

**Question:** Judge from the frame strips of idle, run, dash, and attack combo, and from the still. Which character feels more alive and appealing?

Each strip is 18 frames sampled at 10 frames per second, read left to right and top to bottom, cropped to a 320x240 window around the character at gameplay scale. Judge only the player character. Ignore backgrounds, props, other characters, and on-screen prompts.

- **Pair 01:** both panels show the character standing idle with no input, about 1.8 s.
- **Pair 02:** both panels show the character running in one direction, about 1.8 s.
- **Pair 03:** both panels show the character performing a dash and coming out of it, about 1.8 s.
- **Pair 04:** both panels show the character performing a three-hit melee attack combo from a standstill, about 1.8 s.
- **Pair 05:** both panels show one still frame of the character running in its normal game scene at gameplay scale (silhouette readability).

Note: hurt and death could not be matched (the other side's opening section has no enemies to cause them), so they are not part of this packet.
