# Blind comparison: HUD and in-game UI

**Question:** Mid-fight stills and a damage-taken strip. Which HUD looks more polished and readable?

Judge only the on-screen interface: health, resources and counters, item or upgrade icons, room or level
indicators, damage numbers, banners, and hurt or low-health screen effects. Ask whether each element is
readable at a glance, framed and finished, animated where it changes, and kept clear of the action.
Ignore art style, colour palette, setting, and the game world itself.

Pairs 01 and 02 are full-resolution stills of the whole screen. Pairs 03 and 04 come from one side's only
available footage, a 400x225 recording, so the other side was scaled down to the same 400x225 and both
were then enlarged 2x (pair 03) or 3x (pair 04) with nearest-neighbour scaling. Both sides are equally soft.
Strips read left to right, top to bottom.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: mid-fight with enemies close, the player low on health (one still).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: the moment a room or area is cleared, with the clear banner on screen (one still).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: the player taking damage mid-fight, health dropping (12 frames, 100 ms apart, 1.2 s).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: mid-fight at full health with pickups or currency on screen, at the small recording size (one still).
