# Judging criteria: combat

**Question:** Bursts through a full combo landing on a target. Which hits feel heavier and
more satisfying?

Each pair (`pair-NN.png`) shows the same kind of moment from both games, side by side (A on
the left, B on the right). Judge each pair on hit weight: impact clarity, flash/spark
quality, knockback and recoil, and how alive the target's reaction looks. Then give an
overall verdict for the whole set.

- **pair-01**: A punch/strike connecting with a target at the moment of impact — the first
  clean hit in an attack sequence, with whatever spark, flash, or damage feedback the game
  shows.
- **pair-02**: A heavier, more dramatic hit landing — the moment of peak visual impact
  (biggest flash, most screen-filling effect) in a short attack sequence.
- **pair-03**: A motion strip (6 frames, same rough real-time span) through a hit landing on
  a target, from just before impact to just after — judge the sense of motion and follow-
  through, not any single frame.
- **pair-04**: The aftermath a beat after a short flurry of hits — how the target and
  attacker are posed relative to each other once the exchange settles.
