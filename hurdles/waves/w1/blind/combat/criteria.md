# Blind comparison: combat feel

**Question:** Bursts through a full combo landing on a target. Which hits feel heavier and more satisfying?

Strips are sampled at 10 frames per second, read left to right and top to bottom. Pairs 01 to 03 use a fixed window around the player character, scaled so the character is about the same size on both sides. Pair 04 shows the whole game screen, because the reaction to being hit covers the full screen. Judge how each hit lands: the impact frames, the enemy's reaction, how the third hit compares with the first two, and how clearly you can tell when the player is hit. Ignore art style, colour palette, setting, and on-screen tutorial prompts. Both sides were played live in the browser. One side's frames come from a compressed video recording and are softer. Do not count softness against it.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: a three-hit melee combo against a single enemy that is standing next to the player, from the first swing to the end of the third (12 frames, 1.2 s).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: the frame where the first hit of the combo connects with the enemy (one still).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: the frame where the third and last hit of the combo lands (one still).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: the player being hit by an enemy at close range; the hit lands on the sixth or seventh frame (12 frames, 1.2 s, full screen).
