# Blind comparison: upgrade system

**Question:** Stills of the choice screen and strips of upgrades in action. Which upgrade system looks more exciting?

Strips read left to right, top to bottom. Pairs 02 and 03 are sampled at 15 frames per second (67 ms per frame); pairs 04 to 06 at 10 frames per second (100 ms per frame). Pairs 01 to 03 show the whole game screen. Pairs 04 and 05 use a window around the player character, scaled so the character is roughly the same size on both sides. Pair 06 shows the whole play area on one side and a 2x window around the player on the other, because one side collects pickups across a fixed room and the other across a scrolling field. Both sides were played live in the browser; some upgrades were granted with debug tools so the same kind of moment could be shown. Judge how exciting the upgrade moments are: how the choice is presented, how picking feels, and how visible and spectacular the upgrades are in play. Ignore art style, colour palette, setting, and on-screen key prompts.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: the upgrade choice screen, fully open, with one option highlighted (one still).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: the upgrade choice screen appearing, from the moment it is triggered (16 frames, about 1.1 s).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: the player taking one upgrade and returning to play, starting on the highlighted option (12 frames, about 0.8 s).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: one orbiting upgrade (objects circling the player that hurt enemies) working in a fight, alongside the basic attack (24 frames, 2.4 s).
- **Pair 05:** both panels: the player holding every upgrade at once, fighting (24 frames, 2.4 s).
- **Pair 06:** both panels: currency or experience pickups dropped by enemies being collected by the player (12 frames, 1.2 s).
