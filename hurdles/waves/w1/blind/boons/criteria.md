# Judging criteria: boons

Question: Stills of the choice screen and strips of boons in action. Which upgrade
system looks more exciting?

- **Pair 01**: the full pick-an-upgrade screen, settled, showing three offered
  upgrades side by side with their icons, names, and descriptions.
- **Pair 02**: a close crop on a single offered upgrade, icon plus name plus
  description, at similar zoom on both sides.
- **Pair 03**: a motion strip (8 frames, sequential) of the player fighting with an
  active upgrade/weapon effect visibly firing during combat.
- **Pair 04**: a single dramatic still of an upgrade/weapon effect at full force
  during combat.
