# Run Flow Judging Criteria

## Question
Which run loop feels more complete and replayable?

## Pairs

**Pair 01 (death-moment):** Both panels show the hero at the moment of death or just after falling. Observe the visual treatment: screen effects, timing clarity, and whether the moment feels like a climax or an instant cut-to-menu.

**Pair 02 (summary-screen):** Both panels show the game-over summary screen with final stats (time, kills, boons, cause of death, score). Assess visual design, readability, and whether the screen feels memorable or routine.

**Pair 03 (victory-moment):** Both panels show a late-game or victory moment (the final arena, boss arena, or victory screen). Compare visual presentation, momentum, and the sense of "I want to play again."
