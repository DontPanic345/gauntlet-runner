# Judging question

The first frame, a menu-navigation strip and the settings screen. **Which title screen makes you want to press Start?**

Answer `A`, `B` or `tie` per pair and overall. A tie must mean "genuinely indistinguishable in quality".

# Pairs

- pair-01: both panels: the very first settled frame of the title screen, before any input.
- pair-02: both panels: a strip of 12 frames, 100 ms apart, of the title screen idling with no input (read left to right, top to bottom).
- pair-03: both panels: the main menu, after a couple of keyboard presses.
- pair-04: both panels: a strip of 12 frames, 50 ms apart, while the keyboard presses Down, Down, Up on the main menu (4 frames after each press).
- pair-05: both panels: a strip of 8 frames, 60 ms apart, right after the settings/options screen is opened.
- pair-06: both panels: the settings/options screen, settled.

Note: neither panel can be heard. Judge only what is visible.
