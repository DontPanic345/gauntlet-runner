# Judging criteria: Title screen and menus

**Question**: Which title screen makes you want to press Start? Judge the first frame's visual appeal, the menus' responsiveness and visual feedback, and the settings screen's clarity and interaction feel.

**Pairs**:

1. **Pair 01**: The idle title screen after load, before interaction. Which one draws your eye and makes a stronger first impression?

2. **Pair 02**: A menu item focused (one item highlighted from the start/settings/controls/credits list). Which readability and visual focus indicator works better?

3. **Pair 03**: A motion strip showing menu navigation (cycling through menu items). Which navigation motion feels snappier and more responsive?

4. **Pair 04**: The settings or options screen open. Which one feels more polished and makes adjusting settings feel less tedious?
