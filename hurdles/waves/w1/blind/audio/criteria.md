# Audio Judging Criteria

## Question
Which soundscape is more satisfying and coherent?

## Pairs

**Pair 01 (sfx-moments):** Both panels show spectrograms of isolated SFX events—a melee hit impact, a pickup collection, and an enemy telegraph/attack sound. Pay attention to transient clarity, presence, and distinctiveness of each sound.

**Pair 02 (mixed-combat):** Both panels show spectrograms of the same intensity combat moment: arena room with 3–5 enemies active, hits landing, music playing underneath. Assess clarity, mix hierarchy, and whether telegraph cues remain audible through the density.

**Pair 03 (music-layer):** Both panels show spectrograms of the music track under varying intensity conditions (field music ramping up during combat). Observe layer changes, transient response to intensity shifts, and coherence as the track evolves.
