# Judging criteria — gauntlet corridors

Question: Strips of the chase and trap dodges. Which sequence looks more tense and exciting?

- **Pair 01**: a motion strip of a chase sequence through a hazard-filled corridor, the player fleeing a pursuing threat from left to right.
- **Pair 02**: a still of a trap or hazard moment mid-corridor, close to the player.
- **Pair 03**: an establishing still of a trap corridor before the chase begins, showing the hazards laid out.
