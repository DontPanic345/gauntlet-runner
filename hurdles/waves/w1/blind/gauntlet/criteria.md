# Judging criteria: gauntlet

**Question:** Strips of the chase and trap dodges. Which sequence looks more tense and exciting?

Judge the hazards, how readable and threatening they are, how the player's dodges and dashes read, and how much danger and urgency each sequence conveys. Ignore differences in genre, camera angle, art style and resolution except where they help or hurt that tension.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: a 12-frame strip, 100 ms apart, of the player crossing a section lined with spikes (read left to right, top row first).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: a 12-frame strip, 100 ms apart, of the player dashing past a hazard (read left to right, top row first).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: a 12-frame strip, 100 ms apart, of the player being killed by a hazard (read left to right, top row first).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: a single frame from the middle of a hazard section.

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie`. A tie means genuinely indistinguishable in quality.
