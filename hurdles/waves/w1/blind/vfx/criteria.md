# Blind comparison: VFX and juice

**Question:** Frame strips of hit-spark, death burst and dash. Which effects look more crafted and punchy?

Strips are sampled at 10 frames per second (100 ms per frame), 8 frames each, and read left to right, then top to bottom.
Every panel is a fixed window cut from live gameplay around the action, scaled with nearest-neighbour so the characters are
about the same size on both sides. Judge only the effects: impact flashes, sparks, smears, bursts, trails, rings, debris,
lingering marks, and how each effect is timed (a sharp pop, then a readable decay). Ignore art style, setting, palette,
character design, the weapon type (one game is ranged, the other melee), damage numbers and other UI. One side's frames
come from a compressed animated GIF and are softer and dithered. Do not count that against it.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: the player's attacks landing on an enemy at close range: the impact flashes and sparks on contact (8 frames, 0.8 s).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: an enemy being killed, from the killing blow to what is left a moment later (8 frames, 0.8 s).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: the player character dashing across the floor, from just before the dash to just after it (8 frames, 0.8 s).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: a single frame where a hit is connecting with an enemy (one still).
