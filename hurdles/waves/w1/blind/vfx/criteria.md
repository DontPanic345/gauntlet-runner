# Judging criteria: vfx

**Question:** Frame strips of hit-spark, death burst, dash, spawn. Which effects look more
crafted and punchy?

Each pair (`pair-NN.png`) shows the same kind of moment from both games, side by side (A on
the left, B on the right). Every panel is a 12-frame strip read left to right, top to bottom.
Judge the effects themselves: shape, timing (a fast pop then a decay versus a uniform
spray), colour, readability against the background, and how crafted each frame looks. Then
give an overall verdict for the whole set. One side has no dash, so no dash pair is included.

- **pair-01**: both panels: a player attack landing on an enemy, 12 frames at about 33 ms
  apart, from just before contact through the hit reaction.
- **pair-02**: both panels: an enemy being destroyed, 12 frames at about 33 ms apart, from
  the last moments before death through the burst and what is left behind.
- **pair-03**: both panels: an enemy arriving into the play area, 12 frames at about 66 ms
  apart, from before it is visible until it is fully present.
