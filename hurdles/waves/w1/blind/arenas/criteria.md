# Judging criteria: arenas

**Question:** Looking at these stills of several combat areas and at the strip, which game's levels look more handcrafted and inviting?

Judge the environment (floor, walls, props, lighting, composition, how deliberately each space is laid out) and the room-state moment in the strip. Ignore the player character's own attack effects, the enemy designs and HUD styling, except where they block the view of the level.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: a combat area during a fight, seen in normal play.
- **Pair 02:** both panels: a different combat area during a fight, seen in normal play.
- **Pair 03:** both panels: a third combat area, a stone-floored room with wall lights, during a fight.
- **Pair 04:** both panels: an 8-frame strip, 200 ms apart, of the moment an area is completed and the way onward opens (read left to right, top row first).

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie`. A tie means genuinely indistinguishable in quality.
