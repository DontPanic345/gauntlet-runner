# Blind comparison: boss

Judging question: Intro stills and strips of each phase's signature attack. Which boss
looks more epic and fair?

For each pair, answer which side (A or B) looks more epic and fair, or `tie` if
genuinely indistinguishable in quality. Judge composition, readability of the boss and
its attack, telegraph clarity, and overall visual impact. Do not judge audio (not
present in these stills).

- **pair-01**: the player's first full look at the boss, in the arena, before or as the
  fight begins.
- **pair-02**: a wide, sweeping signature attack in progress, with a clear danger zone
  or trail reaching toward the player.
- **pair-03**: a close-quarters slam/AOE-style signature attack, with a telegraph ring
  or splash around the impact point.
- **pair-04**: a short strip of frames showing combat in motion (several consecutive
  moments of a fight, arranged left to right).
