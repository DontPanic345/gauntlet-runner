# Blind comparison: boss fight

**Question:** Intro stills and strips of each phase's signature attack. Which boss looks more epic and fair?

Judge A against B on each pair, then overall. "Fair" means: can a player see what is coming, where it will land, and when, early enough to get out of the way? "Epic" means presence, spectacle, and a sense of occasion.

Strips are 12 frames read left to right, top to bottom.

1. both panels: the boss revealed at the end of its intro sequence (still).
2. both panels: the boss intro sequence, 12 frames at 250 ms intervals.
3. both panels: a first-phase signature attack from warning to strike, 12 frames at 100 ms intervals.
4. both panels: a later-phase signature attack from warning to impact, 12 frames at 100 ms intervals.
5. both panels: the fight in a late phase (still).

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie` per pair and overall. A tie must mean the two are genuinely indistinguishable in quality.
