# Judging criteria — look

Question: Still frames and a slow pan strip. Which one looks like a finished,
beautiful game?

For each pair, judge which side reads as more finished, readable, and
visually crafted — lighting, palette cohesion, outline/silhouette clarity,
and overall polish. Panels are shown at comparable scale and framing on
both sides.

- **Pair 01**: both panels: a wide establishing view of a dungeon arena, with
  the player character, enemies/creatures, and one or more warm light
  sources visible at once.
- **Pair 02**: both panels: a close-up combat moment — the player character
  striking a nearby enemy, next to an environmental light source.
- **Pair 03**: both panels: a close-up of a single light source (a torch or
  glowing prop) illuminating the floor and a nearby object, with the rest of
  the room falling into shadow.
- **Pair 04**: both panels: a 3x2 grid of frames showing the game in motion
  — camera and action moving through the space over time.
