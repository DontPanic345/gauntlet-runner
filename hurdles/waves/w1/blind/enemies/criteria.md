# Blind comparison: enemies

**Judging question:** Looking at the stills of the enemy cast and the strips of an enemy
attack and an enemy death, which cast is more readable and characterful?

Answer `A`, `B`, or `tie` ("tie" only if genuinely indistinguishable in quality).

Strips are 12 frames, left to right then top to bottom, sampled at about 10 frames per
second on both sides.

- **pair-01**: both panels: a gameplay still of several enemies around the player character in a room.
- **pair-02**: both panels: a closer still of the enemy cast.
- **pair-03**: both panels: a strip of an enemy closing on the player character and attacking.
- **pair-04**: both panels: a strip of an enemy being killed and dying.
