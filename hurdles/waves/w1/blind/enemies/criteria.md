# Blind comparison: Enemies

**Question:** Stills of the cast plus strips of each telegraph and death. Which cast is more readable and characterful?

Strips are sampled at 10 frames per second (100 ms per frame), 8 frames each, and read left to right, then top to bottom.
Every panel is a fixed window cut from live gameplay, scaled with nearest-neighbour so the characters are about the same
size on both sides. Judge the enemies only: how quickly each enemy separates from the floor and from the other enemies,
how distinct and characterful their silhouettes and colours are, how clearly an attack is announced before it lands, and
how satisfying and legible a death is. Ignore the player character's design, the setting and palette as such, the
player's own weapon effects, damage numbers and other UI. One game is a ranged spell-caster and the other is melee; do not
count that either way.

- **Pair 01:** both panels: several different enemy types on screen at once around the player during a fight (one still).
- **Pair 02:** both panels: an enemy announcing an attack (a pose, flash or floor marker) and then committing to it (8 frames, 0.8 s).
- **Pair 03:** both panels: an enemy being killed, from the killing blow to what is left a moment later (8 frames, 0.8 s).
- **Pair 04:** both panels: the player's attacks landing on an enemy and the enemy's hurt reaction (8 frames, 0.8 s).
