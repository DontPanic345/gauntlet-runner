# Judging question

Bursts of starts, stops, turns and dashes around corners. Which movement looks
tighter and more responsive?

# Pairs

1. **Start and stop.** A frame strip: standing still, accelerating to full speed,
   then decelerating back to a stop, in a small enclosed space.
2. **Turn.** A frame strip: the character changes direction while moving, weaving
   past an obstacle.
3. **Dash around a corner.** A frame strip: the character dashes through a
   corridor and rounds a corner, showing whatever trail or afterimage effect
   accompanies the dash.
